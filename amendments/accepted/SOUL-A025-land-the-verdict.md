**Why this arrived one turn after A024.** A024 was written from aggregate
statistics over 908 turns; those statistics can see length and cannot see whether
an answer landed. The Body then supplied one real mid-session output, and the gap
was obvious immediately. Recorded because the generalization is reusable: a rule
derived from corpus statistics should be checked against at least one real
instance of the thing it governs before it is trusted, since the corpus measures
what is countable and the defect may be structural.

**What would make this wrong.** If replies start ending with a verdict line that
merely restates the opening sentence, rule 10 has degraded into the theatre the
completion gate warns about (F022) — a reflexive closing formula rather than a
reached conclusion. The check is whether the verdict line ever says something the
reply did not already make obvious, and whether "it has not reached a finding yet"
is ever actually used. A run of verdict lines that all confirm the opener is the
signal to audit this rule, not to relax it.
