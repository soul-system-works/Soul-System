## Beat 2 — mortality, imminent (custom, 9s)

Motion: a glowing thread draws left-to-right in one pass — a session at work.
Three marks attach: "a decision", "an incident", "a convention". While the
thread is STILL LIT, the line lands above it: "This session is about to end —
these **die** with it." Then the light goes out under it; the line hangs over
the empty frame.
Intent: the warning precedes the death it warns about (Body's spec: imminent,
not retrospective) — it is the hook for the capture sequence that follows.

## Beat 3 — the capture sequence (custom, 24.5s)

Layout: top-left heading `/soul-capture <tag>` (mono, `<tag>` lit); below it
the three tag lines; on the right, a pinned rail of the three store cards —
ideas.md, witness.md, findings/ — which stays for the whole sequence (active
one lit, others dimmed; never animated out and back).

Three identical beats, one per tag — idea, witness, finding. Each: the tag's
line and its store card light while the others dim · a real example enters
bottom-left as a stacked block (ID chip, description BELOW it — max two
lines, never reaching the rail) · ~3.3s read · the chip travels into its
store and stays there as a stamp · the row dims back, the centre clears,
next beat. Examples are REAL record entries, abbreviated:
  **idea** — SOUL-I056 "graduate the theme tokens into the brand" → ideas.md
  **witness** — SOUL-176 "every gate green — still rejected at the watch" → witness.md
  **finding** — SOUL-F038 "cross-project imports silently fail" → findings/

Close: the rail relights in full; the teaching surface (heading + tag lines)
evaporates; "These **survive** the session." with sub "Everything else is
gone."; then the three store cards — stamps riding — glide out of the rail
into a centred horizontal row beneath the sub (the survivors step forward);
~2.6s stillness; a quicker self-fade and a short breath of dark before the
outro (~0.8s tighter than draft4).
